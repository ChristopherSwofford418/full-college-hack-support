Full College Hack

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Applies to Full College Hack: Study on iPhone.

Our approach

Full College Hack is built for personal study. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or create advertising profiles.

Study information

Course names, study goals, imported readings, flashcards, and learning progress are stored on your device. When you choose to create study materials, the course details and text you provide are processed only to complete that request and are discarded from our request service after it completes. They are not used for advertising.

Voice and files

Audio is recorded only when you use the optional recall feature. If you request transcription, the audio is sent directly for that immediate request and is not stored by Full College Hack. Document text is processed only when you import a reading and is not retained by our request service. These optional features are used only to provide the study experience you request.

Diagnostics

This release does not send product diagnostics, identifiers, or usage events from your device. The local diagnostics setting only controls on-device development diagnostics and does not include payment information.

Purchases

College Hack Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. You can manage subscriptions through Apple Account settings.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be updated when we do. See the support page for practical help using the app.
